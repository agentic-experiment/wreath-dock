# wreath-dock

An arcade timing and maritime docking game where you fling drifting wreaths onto pier pegs before the tide carries them away.

### Mechanics & Design

* **The Dock & Tide**: Wreaths drift in from the open water on undulating waves. Click or tap any wreath in the water to launch it toward the nearest pier peg.
* **Peg Matching & Combos**: Successfully landing wreaths on the wooden pier pegs scores points and advances the combo meter. Landing multiple wreaths in rapid succession triggers escalating combo bonuses.
* **Hazards & Tides**: Beware of floating thorn rings and turbulent swells that cost lives upon impact. Keep your dock clear and survive across advancing tide waves.
* **Audiovisual Polish**: Features canvas-rendered water physics, parallax coastal hills, starry night skies, particle bursts on docking, and procedural Web Audio chimes.
